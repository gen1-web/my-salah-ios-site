Common questions

Prayer times look different from my mosque

Open Settings, Prayer times. Pick the calculation method your local masjid follows (the screen shows which suits each region) and choose Standard or Hanafi Asr. You can also adjust each prayer by a few minutes.

The Fajr alarm did not ring

The real alarm needs iOS 26 or newer. Open Settings, Fajr alarm, turn it on and allow alarms when asked. On older iPhones you get a loud time-sensitive notification instead. Check that Focus and notification settings for My Salah allow it.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and search for My Salah. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it and tap Customize.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone only. Use Settings, Back up now to save a file to Files. Read the Privacy Policy.
